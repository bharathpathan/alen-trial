Privacy notice

For the Project ALEN trial page and subscription keys. Last updated 10 November 2026.

What we store

When you create a trial key we store, with the key:

  • your name, email address and role, and your company and intended use if you give them;
  • an account identifier from Firebase Authentication (Google sign-in, or email and password);
  • when the key was created, how long it lasts, and that you agreed to this notice.

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them. This site uses no analytics or advertising cookies.

Why

  • To make one trial per account and to check the key when Project ALEN starts.
  • To show your name and the time left inside the app.
  • To understand who uses Project ALEN, so we can improve it. We do not sell or share your details.

Where

The data is kept in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in the project “project-alen”. In VS Code the key is kept in the editor's secret storage, and your name and the key's end date in a file in your home folder (~/.ai-architect/global-memory.json). The extension does not send your code or project data to us.

How long

Trial records are kept while the product is in trial, and at most 24 months after the key ends, unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

Your choices

  • Remove the key from a machine with Project ALEN: Remove Subscription Key.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by opening an issue on the site's GitHub repository (do not post your key or personal details there; we will reply and verify by email).
