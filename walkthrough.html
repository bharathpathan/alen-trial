Walkthrough: from key to first verified change

Six steps. Follow along in VS Code; each step shows what you will see.

1Get a trial key 2Install the extension 3Activate your key 4Open or import a project 5Connect an AI model 6Make a verified change
Step 1

Get a free trial key

Open the trial page, sign in with Google or with an email and password (we send a link to confirm the address), and fill in a few details. Your key appears straight away, with the exact time it ends.

ALEN-7Q2M…K4ZD7 days left
  • One trial per account. Signing in again shows the same key.
  • Keep the key private: it is how ALEN knows the trial is yours.
Step 2

Install Project ALEN in VS Code

In VS Code, open the Extensions view (Ctrl Shift X, or ⌘ Shift X on a Mac), search for Project ALEN and choose Install.

Project ALEN
AI architecture workbench: model, build, verifyInstall
  • After installing, the Project ALEN icon appears in the status bar with “enter key”.
Step 3

Activate your key

Press Ctrl Shift P (⌘ Shift P on a Mac) and run Project ALEN: Open Workbench. The first time, ALEN asks for your key. Paste it and choose Activate.

> Project ALEN
Project ALEN: Open Workbench

Project ALEN: Show Subscription and Time Left
  • At every start ALEN checks the key, greets you and shows the time left.
  • The time left is always in the status bar and in the account menu at the top right of the workbench.
  • When the trial ends, the workbench stops at the key screen. Paid plans are coming soon.
Step 4

Open or import a project

Open a folder in VS Code. A folder with an ALEN model loads straight away; any other folder goes to Import, where ALEN reads the code and proposes its components, APIs and data. Review the proposal and accept what is right.

Step 5

Connect an AI model

Go to Settings → AI connections and add a provider: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, a local model, or any compatible endpoint. Keys are stored in VS Code's secret storage. ALEN routes each task to the model that does it best, within your budget.

Step 6

Make your first verified change

Open Agent and describe what you want, for example “add pagination to the orders list”. ALEN plans, works in an isolated copy, builds and tests, and hands you a draft with the evidence.
